Earn Time To Play helps gamers balance productivity with entertainment.

The idea is simple: earn time by focusing, spend time by playing.

• Track Focus and Play with quick-add buttons or a live stopwatch
• Keep a time bank balance — play when you have earned it
• See daily progress, history, and analytics charts
• Build a daily tracking streak
• Set a warning threshold, daily play limit, and optional overdraft
• Export your data as CSV
• Fully local-first — your data stays on your device
• Light/Dark mode and English / Traditional Chinese / Simplified Chinese

Guilt-free gaming starts with focused minutes.
